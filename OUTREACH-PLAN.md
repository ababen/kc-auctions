# Associate Auctions: Outreach Plan (Galas + Sellers)

**Goal:** Book KC as the benefit auctioneer for 6+ galas in 2027, and build a steady flow of seller referrals (estates, trusts, investors) from the people who sit at those galas.

**Written:** Nov 19, 2026. **CRM:** GoHighLevel (GHL). Everything below runs in GHL.

**Sources:** public gala listings (Nov 2026), `PLAN.md`, `SOLD-DEALS-RESEARCH.md`, and the Notion KC Daniel project pages (KC's Nov 7 priorities, the proposal draft, the Nov 17 meeting research, and the Nov 19 online-presence snapshot). This plan covers KC's priority #2 (drip / newsletter) and #3 (benefit auction site) for the auction side.

**Why galas first:** A gala puts KC in front of 200–400 wealthy locals, board members, attorneys and realtors, and shows him doing what he's best at: calling bids (2010 Florida Bid Calling Champion). Every gala is also a seller-lead event.
