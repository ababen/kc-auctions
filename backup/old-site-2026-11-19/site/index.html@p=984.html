Public Auction

Wednesday, January 31 at 11 a.m.

Home in Lakefront Private Community in Port St. Lucie

The mobile home is in the deed restricted private community of La Buona Vita CO-OP.  Property will be sold to the highest bidder regardless of price.  Auction will be held at the lakefront clubhouse located at 8601 S Federal Hwy. Port St. Lucie, FL 34952.

Property Information:

  • 490 La Buona Vita Drive Port St. Lucie Florida 34952

  • 2 Bedroom / 2 Bathroom

  • Central Air and Corner Lot

La Buona Vita CO-OP is a 55+ community situated on US 1 in Port St Lucie, FL.  The community comprises 189 homes spread across 45 acres, featuring a 6-acre lake stocked with fish.  Amenities include a clubhouse with a pool, pickleball court, shuffleboard court, card rooms, and a spacious banquet room for special occasions.  To learn more about La Buona Vita, visit their website at www.labuonavita.org.

Terms and Conditions

  • Selling to the HIGHEST BIDDER REGARDLESS OF PRICE.
  • Selling on an “AS IS” basis.
  • 10% Buyer’s premium charges.
  • $10,000 cash or cashier’s check required to bid.
  • Possession at closing.
  • 10% deposit collected day auction, balance of closing within 10 days of auction.
  • Bidder information package available upon request.
  • Auction conducted in the community clubhouse, rain or shine.

For Bidders Information Package or more information email auctioneer at KC@AssociateAuctions.com or call at 772-263-1565.
